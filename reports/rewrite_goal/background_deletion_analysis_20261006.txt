Tools: scratchpad export_bg (read-only per-snapshot background mesh of the 4dmap: getDsgPtr and rawDsg), numpy analysis.

1. Deletion path (code): only ChangeMerger erases background vertices (change_merger.cpp:76-101), and only those marked
   kAbsent (remove_objects_from_background = false, configs/room18_instance_5cm.yaml:249). Real A has no object closure,
   so the marks come from the native RayBackgroundChangeDetector. Its rule (ray_verificator.cpp:142-156, config :222-235):
   - evidence = rays from the sensor to active-window mesh vertices (ray_policy Middle);
   - a ray within 0.08 m of the line of sight whose endpoint lies more than 0.3 m beyond the vertex is "absent";
   - 5 s bins, window 5 bins (25 s); relative absent share > 0.6 -> kAbsent -> erased.
   No incidence angle, no prior, no learned in-place behaviour.

4. Literature (checked against the texts):
   - ERASOR (Lim, Hwang, Myung, RA-L 2021, arXiv 2103.04316): visibility-based removal "has incidence angle ambiguity; as
     the range measurement from the ground becomes longer, the incidence angle of a point becomes more ambiguous, so the
     neighboring ground points may be falsely considered as dynamic objects"; cites Pomerleau 2014 as the Bayesian remedy.
   - Pomerleau, Kruesi, Colas, Furgale, Siegwart, ICRA 2014: per-point binary static/dynamic state, Bayesian update with
     Markov persistence (alpha 0.99 static->static, beta 0.90 dynamic->dynamic, prior 80 % static); update probability
     P(U | theta, phi) falls with the angle between beams and linearly with the incidence angle (1 - 2 phi / pi); depth
     noise eps_d 0.1 m + eps_a 0.2 x range; "the greater the angle between the beam producing q and its surface normal n,
     the less we change the knowledge on q".
   - Curless & Levoy, SIGGRAPH 1996: fusion weights "depend on the dot product between each vertex normal and the viewing
     direction, reflecting greater uncertainty when the illumination is at grazing angles to the surface".
   - Nguyen, Izadi, Lovell, 3DIMPVT 2012: depth noise model valid 10-60 deg (source of P23's 60 deg).
   - DUFOMap (Duberg et al., 2024): a voxel is void only when it and its Chebyshev neighbourhood (radius ~ localization
     error) are observed; voxels within d_s ahead of a hit count as hits (sensor noise margin).
   - Dynablox (Schmid et al., RA-L 2023): free only after a temporal window with the voxel and all neighbours observed.
   - Glossy indoor floors produce LiDAR ghost points below the ground plane (Hwang, Lee, KRoC 2026, symmetric point check).
5. Framework status: the native background rule is outside the unified model (fixed 0.3 m, 0.6 share, 25 s window, no
   incidence, no prior). The object test (eq. 7) already carries P23 (no seen-through verdict beyond 60 deg). The
   phantom surface comes from fusion (online TSDF and session-end refusion integrate the below-floor readings); a
   deletion rule alone would not remove it.
