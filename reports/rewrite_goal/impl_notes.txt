 C1 done (README principle 8, rows w_pm / sensor calibration / zeta / sigma_table, "参数预算"). RangeModel has no valid(): a default model IS the cold start (sigma_table empty = u/sqrt(12),
    w_pm = the mean 1/3 of the Jeffreys prior Dir(1/2,1/2,1/2), zeta 0, sigma_x unknown), and ObservedAbsenceModel forms the posterior of that prior at construction, so every decision has a model
    from the first frame. All skip/throw branches on !valid() are deleted (element_measurement.cpp, evidence_round.cpp, static_surface_selection.cpp, closed_object_background.cpp,

    w_pm: a Dirichlet posterior. The pooled prior is the previous session's posterior mean with the information of one reading, else Jeffreys Dir(1/2,1/2,1/2) (mean 1/3, pseudo-count 3/2);
    the pooled posterior mean adds the expected counts of the EM. Every bin takes the pooled posterior mean as the centre of its prior with one reading and adds its own expected counts. The
    flag RangeModel::w_estimated says whether the weights are a posterior of data (it decides the prior strength; a saved model without the flag is read as estimated iff it has weights).
    The sensor calibration record gives only the sigma_table prior centre: it no longer supplies the depth scale (zeta starts from the previous session's value, else 0).

 R1 numbers that are not model constants: param_registry.csv rows now point to the README's five kinds (dimension of a mathematical object, parameter of a numerical method, engineering format,

 Numerical methods (section 5.1): the EM of w_pm stops when the weights move by at most alpha times their standard error and logs when it reaches its cap (100); the simplex search of the q
    hierarchy stops when the log marginal likelihood varies by at most alpha^2/2 (a parameter within alpha standard errors) and logs the cap (400); Minimum::converged says which. The
    concentration bounds of the q hierarchy are derived: lower bound alpha, upper bound n_max/alpha (n_max = the largest number of decided gaps of any object or class of that gap type, at least 1),
    the mean guard alpha/n_max; an unidentified hierarchy takes the upper bound (complete pooling) instead of the fixed 1e6. The logit quadrature (401 nodes over +-20: mass outside below 1e-8,
    spacing 0.1) is registered as a numerical-method parameter and was not changed.

    markClosedObjectBackground, FramePairDecision (block 5: n, F, ln LR, cut) via an optional out-vector of selectStaticFrames, model::RoundModel::Judgement (deletions of block 8) and ElementTally.

2. README is silent; the most direct reading was implemented (round 6)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
 6.1  Per-bin w_pm of a bin without residuals is the pooled posterior mean; the per-bin EM starts from the pooled value.
 6.2  The EM tolerance uses the standard error sqrt(outlier (1 - outlier)/N) of the outlier share of the set being fitted (N = readings + prior pseudo-count).
 6.3  The previous session's w_pm counts with one reading however many residuals its own posterior had; only its pooled mean is used (the per-bin prior centre is the pooled posterior of this
      session, as the README says), so its per-bin values are not carried as priors.
 6.4  A bin of sigma_table without residuals is its prior centre exactly (previous bin, nearest bin of this session, sensor curve, u/sqrt(12)); with residuals the prior counts as one residual.
 6.5  Concentration bounds: n_max is taken over objects and over classes (sum of their decided gaps) of the gap type being fitted.
 6.6  The cold-start model is formed with the maximum range 0 (the EM is not run without residuals), so it does not depend on R.
