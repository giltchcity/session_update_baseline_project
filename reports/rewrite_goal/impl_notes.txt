1. What changed with the README (this round)
--------------------------------------------
(5e)/principle 3: one loss, two action sets. A commitment that changes persistent state keeps deferral:
  commit H iff Pr >= 1-alpha, commit not-H iff <= alpha, else defer (model::decide/decideLog). An output
  that is recomputed at every publication has no deferral: maximum a posteriori, boundary odds 1
  (model::representationHolds; not a new constant). Applied as below.
  representation: reading classification (6e, unchanged), frames of a reconstruction (6b), whether a
    memory element is shown, identity of a new face, completion, whether a reading enters the refusion.
  commitment: close/confirm a placement, candidate outcomes, delete a record, merge a memory element's
    precision, keep as a different surface, write to the persistent geometry, motion state, a round into
    the statistics.
Principle 4: across sessions sigma_reg = 0 and sigma_x carries the registration error (RangeModel::sigmaEff).
Principle 5: frame-pair selection is MAP; the online write masks a reading without identity by the S_c of its
  semantic class; the frame archive stores identity, class and the native motion mask per pixel and drops
  nothing by class; the session-end refusion fuses a reading without identity iff S_c(t_end - t) >= 1/2
  with the smoothed class estimate; the D1 history depends only on the motion commitment (no displacement
  gate).
Principle 6: rho_1 is projected on [0,1): w = (1-max(0,rho_1))/(1+max(0,rho_1)) in (0,1].
Principle 7: h_o = sqrt(12) sigma_eff(dt_f) and T = 2 h_o for object reconstruction (online, from the
  current estimate) and for the session-end refusion.
Principle 8: the scale is the median-residual minimum over correspondences that are mutual hits at the
  current scale (|e_j| <= delta_+*(dt_j)), alternating to a fixed point from the previous session's scale
  (0 at cold start), the search interval centred on the estimate and doubled when the optimum is on its
  boundary, fine step u/R. Delta_s, sigma_x, pi_dup come from the mixture EM of (12d), not from a scale
  difference or a residual excess.
Principle 9: the prior q_e and the ended distribution of the memory elements are estimated at the end of the
  session by a two-component mixture per group; an element is shown unless its posterior odds of having gone
  are >= 1 (or it is the same surface as a present face with Pr >= 1/2, inside a closed present surface, or
  mostly cut off within the truncation band); its record is deleted only at odds >= (1-alpha)/alpha;
  records that are neither shown nor deleted are kept with their evidence and travel with the evidence state.
Principle 10: (12d) two-component mixture for same-session identity (MAP) and for memory elements (display
  MAP; merge >= 1-alpha; separate >= 1-alpha; else defer).
Principle 12: hidden records and the zeta of earlier sessions are part of the saved state.

3. README is silent; the most direct reading was implemented
------------------------------------------------------------
Carried over from the previous implementation (unchanged unless stated):
a. Pi (5a) by marginal likelihood; (7h) and ended distributions by moment estimators; an ended distribution
   is Beta(1,1) until it has two ended rounds. Element psi: Beta(1/2,1/2) while psi/m is not available.
b. rho_1 is estimated only from >= 2 adjacent pairs of verdicts, w = 1 until then (README: "no adjacent
   pairs": a single pair gives no correlation).
c. Closed-background obligations keep the closure odds and distance of the closed placement.
d. Class id -1 (unknown) is a class like any other group key. A dynamic-only track gate is unbounded until
   the motion statistics exist and the nearest candidate is taken (the maximum of the isotropic motion
   likelihood).
e. A placement born in this session is credited with its observed-in-place time; an inherited placement
   restarts its recursion at its first input. The checkpoint does not store Lambda (recomputed at load).
f. Cold start: until motion/round statistics exist the likelihood ratio is 1 and a round with verdicts
   advances the recursion even if no element is stable yet; the first seconds are biased to the prior.
g. Delta_round of the write commitment is the interval between the last two evidence rounds; no write mask
   before two rounds have run. A pixel without a semantic label (label < 0) has no class to be judged by.
New in this round:
h. Scale: initial half width 10% and coarse step 0.2% are the values of the old rule (README names +-10% as
   the initial interval and 0.02% = u/R for the fine step); the interval doubles at most 8 times; the
   alternation runs at most 10 steps; R of u/R is the sensor's maximum range (the protocol truncation, README
   table 5.1 "R"). Pair residuals of the variogram/noise histograms are e_j at the current scale estimate
   (the sampler applies 1 + zeta to ranges, not to poses). The scale reservoir holds 65536 correspondences.
i. (12d) pairs. Online: the offset d is the reading's residual along the ray from the memory element
   (d = r - rho), rho the range of the reading, base variance sigma_base^2 (sigma_s^2 + broadening), band
   B = T + (|zeta_a| + |zeta_b|) rho with T = 2 h_o of the current estimate (no pair while h_o is not yet
   estimated); a reservoir of 65536 pairs; EM (200 iterations, tolerance 1e-9) once >= 1000 pairs exist.
   Session end: d is the normal offset of the memory element from the nearest same-identity, facing present
   face inside the band; sigma_ee'^2 = element error^2 + present error^2 + (h_e^2 + h_o^2)/12; the fit is
   used only with >= 1000 pairs, otherwise the online estimate stays. Delta_s starts from 0 at load,
   sigma_x and pi_dup from the saved values; |zeta_b| is the largest |zeta| of the earlier sessions
   (psi.zeta_memory, updated when a state is loaded). The same-session identity uses the nearest object face,
   not an explicit argmax over all candidate objects.
j. Principle 9 mixture. Groups: (object element / background element, class), the class being the class of the
   placement or the label of the face's first vertex. EM per group on the frames of its elements (n = frames
   with a verdict + pending ones, F of them see-through), the in-place component being the element's own
   beta-binomial (7s); q has the Jeffreys smoothing (r+1/2)/(N+1); the ended distribution is the weighted
   beta-binomial maximum likelihood (Nelder-Mead, 100 iterations) from the rounds' ended distribution
   (Beta(1,1) at cold start); the EM uses the untempered beta-binomial and the decisions use LR^w. A group with
   fewer than two informative elements takes the fit of all groups pooled. Where no group is identified (no
   informative element or no in-place statistic) nothing is hidden or deleted for lack of evidence; only
   a closed placement's closure odds act as a prior then.
k. Display boundary: log-odds >= 0 hides (README: odds >= 1). Records: an element hidden but not deleted keeps
   (k, j) of its committed history and the pending frames; a round confirmed in place (odds <= alpha/(1-alpha))
   enters the committed history, otherwise the frames stay pending and are counted with the next session's
   frames. Elements that are shown carry no per-face record: the history of a shown background face
   restarts from its construction hits (placement elements keep the registry's histories).
l. Principle 11: the majority is taken over the pixel readings that reach the element (valid readings of
   its footprint, summed over frames), "within the band" meaning the occluder is at most T in front.
m. Principle 7: dt_f is the median gap between adjacent stored frames of the track (online) and the last
   adjacent-frame gap seen by the backend (session end); until the pair scale gamma(0+) exists the native
   resolution setting (object_reconstruction_resolution) is used.
n. Archive layout 3: one 16-bit word per run, class + 1 in the low 15 bits (0: no class) and the motion mask
   in the top bit; a reading with an invalid semantic label is not stored; archives of layouts 1 and 2 still
   load. The S_c rule is applied with the class code; S_c >= 1/2 is evaluated in closed form as
   age <= B (2^(1/A) - 1).
o. Evidence state schema 5 (hidden records, pi_dup, zeta_memory, pair_gamma0; completion outcomes only);
   an older evidence state is read as absent (cold start, README section 7.1).

4. Computation budgets and numerical guards (README table 5.1 where it lists them; approximations)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Persistence prior: hazard grid 300 nodes on ln(lambda) from 1e-9 to 1e3 s^-1; probability grid 401 nodes on
  the logit, bound +-20; concentrations in [1e-2, 1e6]; simplex 400 iterations, tolerance 1e-9.
Hierarchical beta: concentration bisection on ln in [1e-2, 1e6], 80 bisections.
Change filter: log-domain with the exponent capped at 700.
Range model: tan(theta) guarded at grazing incidence (1e-6).
Sensor calibrator: range bins 0.5 m over 0..8 m, 6 incidence bins, 12 geometric time-difference bins, residual
  cells of 0.5 mm over +-0.5 m, >= 1000 samples per cell, scale reservoir 65536 (64 frames of about 1000
  stride-16 pixels), band-pair reservoir 65536, EM of w_pm 100 iterations / 1e-10.
Frame pairs: every 16th pixel, partner frames at offsets 1,2,4,.. frames; frame-pair attribution 512 pixel
  samples per pair (standard error <= 2.2 %).
Motion model: newest 512 frames per object, refit every 64 added frames.
Memory mixture: EM at most 100 iterations (tolerance 1e-7), ended-distribution search 100 simplex
  iterations on the pairs with responsibility above 1e-9 of the ended mass; concentration bounds [1e-2, 1e6].
Free space V_free: at most 256 evenly spaced frames, every 8th pixel; margin delta_+ at the largest drift.
Winding number: 64 Fibonacci directions.
Math-preserving optimisations: log-domain recursion and interval; odds instead of probabilities; sparse voxel
  hashing of V_free; parallelFor with per-thread records; packed element-cell keys; distinct (n, F) pairs in
  the mixture; precomputed pair geometry in the scale search (one norm per pair and node).
