3. README is silent; the most direct reading was implemented
------------------------------------------------------------
a. Hyperparameter estimators. Pi (5a) is fitted by marginal likelihood as stated. The hierarchical beta
   of (7h) and the ended distributions use moment estimators (phi from the within-object scatter, kappa
   from the between-object scatter, mu the pooled mean) instead of a marginal-likelihood search; an ended
   distribution is Beta(1,1) until it has two ended rounds.
b. w = (1-rho1)/(1+rho1) exactly as in (7) (CODE_FIX round 1 removed the cap at 1); rho1 is bounded
   away from +-1 by 1e-6 only to keep the quotient finite, and is estimated only from >= 2 adjacent
   pairs of verdicts, so w = 1 until then.
c. Element psi: Beta(1/2,1/2) cold prior where psi / m is not yet available.
d. Pair and fill statistics (9) are global; pi_dup counts only memory-present pairs.
e. Closed-background obligations keep the closure odds and the distance of the closed placement.
f. Class id -1 (unknown) is a class like any other group key; the background q^g is the Jeffreys
   posterior mean of the background gap outcomes.
g. A dynamic-only track gate (principle 5) is unbounded until the motion statistics exist; (5e)
   "no evidence" for a candidate uses the projected placement odds.
h. sigma_reg(dt) is applied to cross-session elements with t_e taken as the session start.
i. A placement born in this session is credited with its observed-in-place time (Lambda_0 = 0, the
   Jeffreys posterior is then normal); an inherited placement restarts its recursion at its first input.
j. The checkpoint does not store Lambda; it is recomputed at load as Lambda_0 of the gap (15).
k. Cold start: until motion / round statistics exist the likelihood ratio is 1 (prior hazard only) and
   a round with verdicts advances the recursion even when none of its elements is yet stable; the
   first seconds of a session are therefore biased towards the prior.
m. Delta_round of the write commitment (principle 5, assumption 4) is the interval between the last
   two evidence rounds of the backend; no write mask is applied before two rounds have run.
n. Scale correspondences (9b) are associated when the raw residual (ζ = 0) lies within the background
   truncation of the map (README principle 8, "关联≤背景截断"); the scale is the median-residual
   minimum of a coarse search (±10 %, step 0.2 %) followed by a fine search (step 0.02 %) around the
   coarse optimum; ties go to the smaller |ζ|. The scale reservoir holds 65536 correspondences
   (64 frames of about 1000 stride-16 pixels).
o. Cold start: with no previous state and no default error file psi is estimated from the first frame
   pairs of the session; no source is consumed until psi is valid (runEvidenceRound).
p. Default ids: elements of unidentified endpoints use class id -1 and the foreign-label channel (c,d).

4. Computation budgets and numerical guards (README table 5.1 where it lists them; approximations)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Persistence prior: hazard grid 300 nodes on ln(lambda) from 1e-9 to 1e3 s^-1 (support of the integral;
  no fitted or posterior rate lies near it); probability grid 401 nodes on the logit, bound +-20; concentrations in [1e-2, 1e6]; simplex 400 iterations, tolerance 1e-9.
Hierarchical beta: concentration bisection on ln in [1e-2, 1e6], 80 bisections.
Change filter: log-domain with the exponent capped at 700 (range of a double).
Range model: tan(theta) guarded at grazing incidence (1e-6).
Sensor calibrator: range bins 0.5 m over 0..8 m, 6 incidence bins, 12 geometric time-difference bins,
  residual histogram cells of 0.5 mm over +-0.5 m, >= 1000 samples per cell, scale-pair reservoir 65536,
  EM 100 iterations / tolerance 1e-10.
Frame pairs (calibration): every 16th pixel of the newest frame, partner frames at offsets 1,2,4,..
  frames (one per time bin). Frame-pair attribution (static_surface_selection): 512 pixel samples per
  pair (standard error of a proportion <= 2.2 %).
Motion model: newest 512 frames per object, refit every 64 added frames, correlation bound 1e-6.
Free space V_free: at most 256 evenly spaced frames, every 8th pixel, sparse voxel records with the
  session key; the margin is delta_+ at the largest registration drift.
Winding number: 64 Fibonacci directions.
Performance optimisations that preserve the mathematics: log-domain evaluation of the recursion and of the
  credible interval; odds kept instead of probabilities (avoids 1-alpha rounding in the thresholds);
  sparse voxel hashing of V_free; per-frame parallel loops (parallelFor) with per-thread records merged
  afterwards; element histories keyed by the packed map-resolution cell.
