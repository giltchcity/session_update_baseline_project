1. What changed with the README (c5cfd18 -> 2f34864) and where it lives now
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The model of c5cfd18 (change-point posterior with a hazard prior, learn-after-commit, element-level beta-binomial,
two-component EM for displaced copies, variogram widening of the hit band, resolution derived from noise) is replaced by the
structure of 48a3033 with derived numbers:
 - principle 3: the end of a placement is the CUSUM (5r) of look ratios, weight w_n = first-judged share of the reliable
   samples, threshold ln((1-alpha)/alpha), cleared when C returns to 0; closing also needs the ray majority (contradicting rays
   more than supporting rays since the last look that directly saw the placement in place). model/change_filter.h (Cusum),
   state/persistent_object_state.cpp resolveRound.
 - principle 6: reliable samples (7s) h_e >= k_min, v_e = 0 (all samples vote before the placement was directly seen in place in
   the session); look ratio (7) one-sided against the in-place distribution (m, c) of (7h) (3 pseudo-looks, scatter floor,
   c >= 2; cold start m_0 with c = 2); learned after judging, only from looks that directly saw the placement in place;
   k_min from eps^k <= alpha. model/round_model.*, evidence/element_measurement.cpp, state/persistent_object_state.cpp.
 - principle 2: q_{c,g} for three gap types (continuous, within session, session), empirical-Bayes Beta-binomial, Jeffreys cold
   start; only the silent case of principle 13 reads it. model/persistence_prior.*.
 - principle 4/8: sigma_eff^2 = sigma_table(rho)^2 + (h tan th)^2/12 + sigma_x^2 (no time-difference term), b = zeta_now rho -
   zeta_e rho_e; sigma_table is the robust residual scale of the readings against the fused surface (online: the background
   mesh against the newest stored frame each round; session end: the present surface), per range bin with nearest-bin fill;
   w_pm by EM pooled then per bin; zeta by the alternation (kept); sigma_x from the offsets of previous-session surfaces.
   model/range_model.*, model/sensor_calibrator.*, evidence/frame_pair_sampler.cpp, adapters/backend_session.cpp.
 - principle 5: D1 start and settling are CUSUMs without a prior on the share of the object's pixels in motion clusters
   (tracker); the static frames are chosen by the maximum a posteriori of the pair ratio (no q_ij); the native integration
   mask is the only exclusion of readings (no class or hazard write gate).
 - principle 7: h_o is the native object_reconstruction_resolution (the psi-derived resolution and the variogram are gone).
 - principle 9/10/11: the session-end retention is (10b) see-through > hit hides; the record is deleted only when the frames as
   one look give ln LR >= ln99 (class in-place distribution); identity within one voxel of the current object surface;
   displaced copy window (12d); INSIDE by back > front among hitting directions; representation-resolution constraint;
   completion by any reading within tau = max(h_o/2, sigma_table) (14f). The memory mixture, the element posterior, the
   duplicate-mixture EM and the closure-odds prior of background copies are removed. surface/session_refusion.cpp,
   surface/closed_object_background.cpp.
 - principle 12/13: registry state (C, sample records {h_e, v_e, rho_e}, zeta_e per placement, look statistics, gap counts) is
   saved; a restored placement starts with C = 0 and no reliability record; candidates are settled by (a)-(g).
Removed files: model/{hierarchical_beta,duplicate_mixture}.*, model/change_filter.cpp (the header is the Cusum),
surface/memory_mixture.*; surface/element_posterior.cpp is only the tally header's translation unit.

3. README is silent; the most direct reading was implemented
-------------------------------------------------------------
 1. A "look" is one evidence round of a placement; a round "directly saw the placement in place" if at least one of its frames
    did (frame: own-identity hit samples >= k_min and more than the see-through samples; every sample of the placement counts).
 2. A sample is a cell of the map resolution (a face centre per cell), at most 1500 per placement (README table 5.1 budget), taken
    deterministically evenly in cell order.
 3. The denominator of w_n for a placement never directly seen in place in this session is the number of samples queried;
    afterwards the number of samples with h_e >= k_min and v_e = 0.
 4. The fraction learned from a look is F/n of its reliable verdicts (looks without a reliable verdict are not looks).
 5. m_0 of a look is the mean of the see-through rates the measurement model predicts for its reliable readings. The population
    of (7h) needs at least two objects with >= 3 in-place looks (a scatter needs two); without it the cold start is (m_0, c = 2),
    i.e. v_0 = m_0(1-m_0)/3.
 6. eps = (foreign + 1/2)/(labelled + 1) pooled over directly seen frames (Jeffreys); with no labelled hit k_min = 7. Only hits
    that carry a physical identity are labelled (background and unidentified are "missing", principle 4).
 7. Numerical guards only: m clamped to (1e-9, 1-1e-9); concentration capped at 1e6 if the scatter is 0.
 8. The in-place distribution of a memory face or background copy at deletion is that of its class (pooled looks of the objects of
    the class shrunk to the population), the class being that of its placement or its vertex label; background faces use
    m_0 of their frames.
 9. Ray majority (principle 3): the contradicting ray is a ray classified T (passes the sample), the supporting one a ray classified
    H that lands on the placement's own identity, one per (frame, sample), counted since the last look that directly saw the
    placement in place (the counters restart there and that look's rays are not counted). The native radial 0.08 m / depth
    0.3 m tolerances remain those of the unchanged native ray detector; the registry uses the model's own delta_* bands.
10. Gap type (principle 2): session for the first decisive look of a restored placement; continuous if the previous round was the
    placement's last decisive look; within-session otherwise. A gap is decided by the decisive look after it: directly seen in place
    -> not changed; ended (CUSUM closure, accepted native motion) -> changed.
11. (f): "built from many observations" is reconstruction_frames >= k_min; "not overlapping the entity" is non-intersecting
    bounding boxes; "no own-identity support since its last support" is support_rays == 0 since the last look that directly saw it.
12. (g): n of the candidate = its sampled cells; "majority away" = more than half of them farther than the cross-session band
    (delta_+ with sigma_x at the range of the candidate centroid in its last frame, plus |zeta_now - zeta_e| rho) from the
    inherited surface; decisive count n* from the class in-place distribution; q is the session-gap q of the class.
13. (5o): E_{h->o} are the verdicts of all samples of the candidate in the frames while h was in place (as before); E_{o->h} are
    the reliable samples of h in the frames of the candidate; both enter as look ratios (7); silent evidence leaves q/(1-q).
14. Cross-session samples are not judged before sigma_x is estimated: the frames of such a round stay unconsumed (the watermark is
    not advanced). sigma_x online is estimated once >= 1000 band pairs exist (the MAD budget of table 5.1) from the offsets
    d - b of readings of previous-session samples inside T + (|zeta_now| + |zeta_e|) rho; at the session end from the offsets of the
    memory faces from the present surface of the same identity (64 evenly sampled frames give the nearest range).
15. rho_e of a placement's samples is the running mean of the ranges of own hits in directly seen frames (placements made in this
    session only); zeta_e per placement is this session's zeta when saved; a record that is missing means rho_e = the reading's
    range and zeta_e = the previous session's zeta. Faces carry (hits, see-throughs, rho_e, zeta_e) in surface_error.bin v4 and in the
    hidden records (v3 files are read, their pending arrays ignored).
16. Online sigma_table: each round takes the newest stored frame against the background mesh (vertices facing the sensor by the
    winding of the mesh, at most 32768 evenly spaced vertices); the truncation band is the background truncation.
17. w_pm per bin: Dirichlet prior of total weight 1 (the Jeffreys analogue) centred on the pooled estimate.
18. Unidentified readings at the session end: the native motion mask is the only exclusion; surfaces the later frames see through
    leave the present by the constant-weight averaging of the TSDF (the majority of (10b) at voxel level).
19. If the online estimator had no outlier weights at the session end, w_pm are the Jeffreys-smoothed outlier frequencies of the
    present-surface residuals; sigma_table is the present-surface scale where it has an estimate, else the online one.
20. (12d): rho is the range of the nearest reading that reaches the face, 2h is one layer voxel, tau = max(h, sigma_table(rho)); the
    present surface must be of the same identity, with an agreeing normal, on the normal side of the memory face.
21. (c) additionally requires the face to be seen in this session and farther than one voxel from the object's present surface.
22. Completion candidates are the online-map faces whose centroid cube has an unintegrated corner (unchanged); the readings that count
    are those after the face's own last observation.
23. B04 background copies: candidates are background vertices within sqrt(3) voxels of a placement committed ended (retrieval);
    the evidence is recomputed from all frames after the vertex's own last observation each round.
24. Tracker: the per-frame ratio is neutral until a static frame has been learned; a frame is learned as static when the CUSUM is 0
    after it; the cold mean of (7h) there is the Jeffreys-smoothed pooled share of the learned static frames.
25. Frame-pair selection (principle 5): the in-place distribution of the object with m_0 of the carried pixels; ratio >= 1 cuts.
26. Registry checkpoint schema 6; older schemas restore the geometry and start the prior and the look statistics cold.

4. Computation budgets and numerical guards
--------------------------------------------
1500 samples per placement; 16 range bins of 0.5 m, 0.5 mm residual cells, >= 1000 samples per bin; 32768 mesh vertices per
residual pass; 65536-pair reservoirs; EM 100 iterations; zeta alternation <= 10; 64 evenly sampled frames for the nearest range of
sigma_x; 64 Fibonacci directions; simplex/grid budgets of the gap hierarchy; decisive-sample search <= 100000.
