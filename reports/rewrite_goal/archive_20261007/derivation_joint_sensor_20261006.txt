==============================================================================================
1. Model
==============================================================================================
1.1 Data of one look. For every reliable sample (the same set as now), take its latest outcome in the round:
    the latest measured verdict d in {S on-surface, T seen-through} if it has one (exactly the verdicts f
    is built from now); otherwise d = N, if the sample was in view (facing) with no valid depth in the round.
    Occluded readings give no outcome, as now. Each outcome carries the segmentation result s in {own, other}
    at that reading. A look is then the six counts n_{d,s}.

1.2 Factorisation (rule 1 of 05:5x):
        p(look | H) = p_depth(look | H) * prod_{d in S,T,N} p(s_d | d, H),     H in {P in place, A absent},
    where s_d = n_{d,own} / n_d is the own-label share among the samples with depth outcome d.
    - p_depth is eq. (7) word for word: f = T/(S+T), the one-sided share density, kMinSamplesInView,
      first-judgment weights, Page, log 99, judge-then-learn with gate (i), the population and cold start.
      Nothing in it is touched.
    - Assumption A1: the count of no-depth samples is ancillary, i.e. it has the same distribution under P
      and A. The current depth factor already makes this assumption by ignoring N. It is stated here because
      the label factor conditions on N.
    - Assumption A2: given the hypothesis and the depth outcomes, the three label shares are independent.
      This is the neutrality of the generalised Dirichlet / Dirichlet tree (Connor & Mosimann 1969, JASA
      64:194-206; Minka 1999, "The Dirichlet-tree distribution"). It is used only for the label nodes. The
      depth factor is not re-modelled, and no 6-class Dirichlet is used, because it does not degenerate
      exactly when segmentation is uninformative (rule 1).

1.3 Label nodes (one Beta per node d, same estimator family as the depth share):
    - A node is scored when its parent count n_d >= min(30, reliable). This is the P25 rule, with the same
      constant and source as the depth share. Below that, r_d = 1.
    - In-place model P_{j,d}: the object's in-place looks, shrunk towards the label population (per-object
      means) with kRobustLooks pseudo-looks. Robust scatter, variance floor 1e-4, concentration floor 2,
      share clamps 0.005/0.995: all the presentMoments/presentBeta forms, with their existing sources. It
      learns after scoring, on the same gate (i).
    - Cold start: tied. While no label population exists, r_d = 1. No category is assigned in advance to
      "speaks for presence" or "speaks for absence".
    - Absent model A_d (supervisor 05:5x, point 1). If the object is gone, its own label appears at its site
      only by a false alarm of the segmenter. A_d is therefore the false-alarm law of node d (Rosen et al.
      2016, P_F; Dawid & Skene 1979). It is learned online from every in-place look of every state: the
      share of that state's samples with depth outcome d that carry a FOREIGN physical identity, i.e. a
      surface confirmed to be something else that carries another object's label.
        * It is pooled over states, as a sensor property, with the same population estimator. It is not per
          identity: per-identity false alarms are too rare to estimate.
        * Counting any foreign identity makes it an upper bound on the rate for one specific identity, so it
          errs on the side of weaker presence evidence.
        * It needs no closure, so it is active in fresh sessions (real A, Bonn).
        * Cold start: tied (r_d = 1 until the pooled law exists).
      The uniform convention is NOT used for the label nodes. Supervisor's data: with a uniform absent side,
      the A-end chair, which is still in place, gets +1.7 / +2.6 / -0.6, the wrong direction, because the
      uniform law drops the fact that an absent object's label almost never appears.
      Replaced earlier form (used in the replays of secs. 2-3, because the foreign shares are not logged):
      a pool of the shares of closed windows, from Page's argmax candidate s^ (Page 1954; Hinkley 1971,
      Biometrika 58:509-523) to the commit, shrunk towards P_{j,d}. It is inactive until a closure exists,
      and its windows include move-spanning looks (pool mean 0.18-0.24), which weakens the evidence.
    - The comparison is two-sided: r_d = Beta(s_d; P_{j,d}) / Beta(s_d; A_d), and r = prod_d r_d.
    - Share form, not counts. Each node contributes one share per look, like f. A Beta-binomial over the
      samples of one depth class treats hundreds of correlated samples as independent. That is the P28
      failure mode (sec. 5c) and the source of magnitudes such as -416 to -723 or +34 to +55 for single
      looks. In share form with the mixture of 1.4, a single look is bounded by the learned rates.

1.4 Look-level confusion of the segmentation channel (supervisor 05:5x, point 2). Sources: Dawid & Skene
    1979, Applied Statistics 28:20-28 (observer error rates learned by EM without ground truth); Rosen, Mason
    & Leonard ICRA 2016 (the detector's miss and false-alarm rates are properties of the sensor).
    With probability P_M (= rho) a look of a present object shows the label pattern of an empty site
    (whole-look miss: own share at the false-alarm level). With probability P_F (= rho_F) a look of an empty
    site shows the object's pattern (whole-look false alarm). Then
        p(labels | P) = (1-P_M) L_P + P_M L_A,     p(labels | A) = (1-P_F) L_A + P_F L_P,
        llr_lab = log[(1-P_F) + P_F r] - log[(1-P_M) r + P_M]          (absent : present; r = L_P / L_A).
    Properties (exact algebra):
      (a) r = 1 gives llr_lab = 0, for any P_M, P_F.
      (b) P_M + P_F = 1 (an uninformative detector: identical confusion rows) gives llr_lab = 0, for any r.
      (c) Per look, llr_lab lies in [log(P_F/(1-P_M)), log((1-P_F)/P_M)]. With P_M = P_F the bounds are
          those of Rosen's binary detection, y = 0 or 1.
      (d) P_M = P_F = 0 gives -log r, the unbounded product of node ratios.

1.5 Learning rho and rho_F. The look's label verdict is y = 1[log r < 0] (labels favour absence; looks with
    r = 1 carry no label information and are excluded).
      rho   = (#{depth favours presence, llr_depth < 0, and y = 1} + 1) / (#{llr_depth < 0} + 2)
              (supervisor's set: depth says in place, own label missing).
      rho_F = (#{look of a closed window and y = 0} + 1) / (#{looks of closed windows} + 2).
    Cold start: Laplace's rule of succession (k + 1)/(n + 2) (Laplace 1814). That is the uniform Beta(1, 1)
    prior, the same pseudo-count 2 as kUniformPriorPseudoCount. Both rates are pooled over states and carried
    across sessions.
    Why rho_F does not use the depth sign: the depth factor has a known tail of static objects at f 0.5-0.8
    (experiments row 94). Counting those looks, where the labels are right, as label false alarms would
    raise rho_F and cut the presence bound exactly where it is needed. Closed windows are confirmed by the
    rays (C > S). This is a decision-directed / classification-EM step of D&S (Celeux & Govaert 1992, CSDA
    14:315-332).
    Long-run property (the condition for the supervisor's "a single miss never commits"):
      - one whole-look miss gives at most log((1 - rho_F) / rho);
      - on its own that exceeds log 99 only if rho < (1 - rho_F) / 99;
      - the replay learns rho 0.13-0.28, bound 1.2-1.9.
    A sensor that really never misses would, correctly, be trusted more.
    RECORD ONLY, not candidates (review 06:2x: no choosing a form by its result):
      - learnconf 1, a decision-directed P_M on a provisional in-place set with prior 0.05 x 3
        pseudo-looks;
      - fixed P_M = P_F = 0.05.
    Both move the cold-start alpha and kRobustLooks into a different role, which breaks the same-role rule
    for borrowed constants. Only the Laplace (Bayes-Laplace Beta(1,1)) form above is a candidate.
    Under it, B inst13 397.95 is not removed; this is reported as such.

1.6 Total per look (revised after review 06:2x). For candidate s,
        page[s] += w_s * llr_depth + w_lab * llr_lab.
    The depth term and its first-judgment weights w_s are unchanged; exact degeneracy only needs that.
    The label term's correlation across looks is derived separately and is NOT inherited from the depth
    weights. Those weights handle depth errors that persist per sample and per viewpoint (edge misalignment,
    geometry offset). For detections, the persistence filter's standard assumption (Rosen et al. 2016) is
    that looks are conditionally independent given the state.
    Two treatments, both replayed on the Stage 0 logs:
      (i) independent looks: w_lab = 1;
      (ii) correlated whole-look misses: w_lab = 1 / deff, the effective-sample factor (Kish 1965, design
           effect). For a lag-1 autocorrelated sequence, deff = (1 + rho_1) / (1 - rho_1) (Bayley &
           Hammersley 1946, JRSS Suppl. 8:184-197).
             * rho_1 is the online lag-1 autocorrelation of the label verdict y between consecutive
               depth-favoured looks of the same state, pooled over states.
             * It is 0 (independence) until kRobustLooks pairs exist.
             * It is taken in [0, 0.99]: deff >= 1, i.e. no negative correlation that would inflate the
               evidence.
    Stage 0 data test the assumption: the lag-1 autocorrelation and the runs of whole-look misses of in-place
    looks (Wald & Wolfowitz 1940 runs test).
    Unchanged: Page, the log 99 commit, P14 logit q, and the rays C > S. With uninformative labels
    llr_lab = 0 in both treatments, so degeneracy is unaffected.

1.7 Withdrawn from this step:
    - The hierarchical posterior predictive (derivation A, derivation_inplace_robust_20261006.txt). It would
      change the depth factor, which rule 1 forbids.
    - The 6-class Dirichlet (rule 1).

2. Exact degeneracy (rule 2) - proof and replay
==============================================================================================
Labels "all other" at the input of the label factor (one class "other" replaces every label there) make
every own share and every foreign share 0. Then:
  - the label population (per-object means) is 0, with scatter at the floor;
  - every P_{j,d} has mean 0 and variance at the floor;
  - the false-alarm law A_d is 0 with variance at the floor, i.e. the same Beta (the same estimator on equal
    inputs). The replaced pool form likewise gives A = P.
So r = 1 and, by (a), llr_lab = 0 bit for bit. Before the populations exist r = 1 (tied cold start).
Tied models (P_M = 1, P_F = 0 makes the factor identically 1) give llr_lab = 0 by (a)/(b).

Replay, all 8 sessions (real A, B, C; Bonn moving, placing, removing; synthetic A, B), with the pool form of
A (the false-alarm form needs Stage 0 data; the algebra above is the same):
  - cother and lother (all other): closures AND commits identical to L2_FINAL2 in all 8.
  - ctied: identical in all 8.
  - The depth-only replay reproduces the logged llr (max deviation 1.2e-5 to 4.8e-4).

Shuffled labels (label shares permuted within each session, i.e. drawn independently of the hypothesis):
  - Not bit-identical, and no learned model can be. The per-object in-place estimate and the pooled absent
    estimate are two finite samples from one distribution, so r differs from 1 by sampling noise. Property
    (b) only holds in the limit, as P_M + P_F tends to 1.
  - Measured with 5 seeds (traced objects).
    Laplace form (learnconf 2):
      max |llr_lab| per look: 0.54-0.87;
      rho + rho_F at session end: 0.80-0.98, tending to 1;
      closures identical in all 8 sessions for 3 of 5 seeds;
      2 of 5 lose real C inst 2.
    Decision-directed form (learnconf 1):
      max |llr_lab| per look: 0.47-1.67 (p95 0.23-0.74);
      P_M + P_F at session end: 0.75-0.89;
      with real labels, by contrast: max 3.58, P_M 0.16, P_F 0.068.
  - Decisions differ only where L2_FINAL2 sits within that margin of log 99 (4.595):
      real C inst 2 (L2_FINAL2 statistic 4.60 at 56.8 s, margin 0.005): closure lost;
      real B monitor rec 22 (L2_FINAL2 4.55): closure added in 1 of 5 seeds (learnconf 1 only);
      real B inst 7 rec 11: earlier in 1 of 5 seeds (learnconf 1 only).
  - Real A, Bonn x3 and the synthetic closures were identical in every seed (synthetic: commit-only
    differences).
  - So "never worse than now" cannot be guaranteed for a marginal L2_FINAL2 decision. The deviation is
    bounded per look and shrinks as P_M + P_F tends to 1.

4. Cascade (rule 3)
==============================================================================================
A changed closure changes the mobility counts (changes/continuations) of that identity and of its semantic
class. That changes q (stateChangeProbability, Beta-Bernoulli over own, class and group counts), which enters:
  - the commitment of inherited states (logit q + page[0]);
  - the P02/P03 same-site / different-site comparisons with the M3 posterior.
Changed closures (proposed form):
  - real B: +inst7 rec22 (false), -inst13 rec28 (false removed).
  - real C: none added or removed, only earlier.
  - synthetic: none added or removed.
Direct effect on q in real C, if the B relation counts for the C root state:
  - inst7: changes 1 -> 2, q 0.76 -> 0.82 (logit +0.37).
  - inst13: changes 3 -> 2, q 0.576 -> 0.529 (logit -0.19); or 2 -> 1, 0.509 -> 0.46.
  - Both C commitments of these states have margins far above that: inst7 rec11 statistic 14.4, inst13 rec2
    7.9. They do not move in the replay.
  - The class populations change as well: class 74 (inst 20) and class 115 (inst 12, 16, 17).
The L1 replay holds q at the logged values and cannot evaluate P02/P03. The L2 run must therefore report the
MOBILITY_PRIOR q of inst 7, 13, 12, 16, 17 and 20 in C, and every TOP_SAME_SITE_CANDIDATE / TOP_CLOSE /
SESSION_CLOSE / EXTENT decision that differs from L2_FINAL2. Real B also changes through the monitor
closure: it closes and is rebuilt, which affects B's online object metrics and G1/G2.

5. Risks (rule 4)
==============================================================================================
a. Object present, segmentation misses, depth also wrong.
   - The label term adds at most log((1-P_F)/P_M) per look, multiplied by the look's weight:
       0.69 at the Laplace cold start (2.94 with the 0.05 prior);
       1.2-1.9 with the learned B/C rates.
   - A false closure appears earlier or new when the depth-only statistic is within that amount of log 99.
   - Observed: exactly this case once, the real B monitor rec22. Depth-only 3.47 at 44.2 s; s_S 0.14 because
     the reconstructed surface is off the monitor (the deferred geometry TODO); +1.28 crosses.
   - Conversely, the label term removes the real B inst13 397.95 false closure (s_S = 1.0, -3.45) under
     learnconf 1. Under the Laplace form it does not (-1.8, rho_F 0.125).
   - The supervisor's simulated whole-look miss (chair +37 / +55, Bonn +27 / +39 in count form) is bounded
     in this form by log((1-rho_F)/rho) per look. It cannot commit on its own while rho > (1-rho_F)/99.
b. Object moved, residual or late labels at the old site.
   - Presence evidence is at most log((1-P_M)/P_F) per look: 2.5-3.8 with the learned rates.
   - It lowers only the Page candidates that include the residual looks; candidates after them start from
     0. So the delay is at most the residual period plus the looks the depth term needs from there.
   - Latest-verdict semantics already removes the frames before the move inside one round: the C inst2
     move look gives s_S 0.379 and +1.49.
   - Observed: no closure later than L2_FINAL2 in any of the 8 sessions. The only lost closure is B inst13
     rec28, which is GT static.
c. P28 identity channel (L2_P8: real C P 96.55, inst2 about 70 s late, one D2 missed). Differences:
   (1) It was a count likelihood, Beta-binomial over ~500 correlated samples treated as independent. Here
       there is one share per look per node, like f.
   (2) It counted own identity "at any time in the round": 435/508 at the move look, giving -46.9 and a Page
       reset. Here the label is the one at each sample's latest verdict, conditional on the depth outcome:
       s_S 0.379.
   (3) Its laws were constants fitted on one real B run (aP 1.19, bP 0.77, aE 0.136, bE 46.8). Here both
       laws are learned online, and the absent law is tied to the in-place law until absences are observed.
   (4) It was unbounded. Here each look is bounded by the learned confusion (1.4).
   (5) It had no exact degeneracy (sec. 2 holds here).
   In the replay the same C inst2 look now gives +1.49, and the state closes at 37.7 s = final10's 461.1.
   The P28 failure mode (-46.9 on the move look) cannot recur: the per-look minimum is
   log(P_F/(1-P_M)) ~ -2.4.

  - "Not worse than now in every case" cannot be promised for decisions that L2_FINAL2 itself takes within
    the per-look bound of log 99 (sec. 2, shuffle).
