P07 exact observation-law review after explicit resume

Actual source: persistent_object_state.cpp motionGeometryBayesFactor and offStateShare.
The implementation retains f=sum_i p_off_i/N and n_eff=N^2/sum_c N_c^2,
then uses theta^(n_eff*f)(1-theta)^(n_eff*(1-f)) as a Beta update kernel.

1. The effective count is a variance summary, not a likelihood sufficient statistic.
Under the exact perfect-within-cell, independent-between-cell binary model already
invoked in README, cell layouts (3,3) and (4,1,1) both have N=6 and n_eff=2.
For the possible observation f=0, the actual likelihoods are (1-theta)^2 and
(1-theta)^3, respectively. At theta=1/2 the probabilities are 1/4 and 1/8.
Their identical passed summary cannot identify both exact observation laws.
This is an information counterexample under the stated model, not a code-test
failure or a new algorithm trial.

2. Fractional count does not admit the ordinary parameter-independent binomial measure.
For fixed n>0 let T=n*f in [0,n]. If a finite positive measure nu independent of
theta normalized the bare kernel for every theta, then substituting
 theta=exp(t)/(1+exp(t)) gives
 integral exp(t*T) nu(dT) = (1+exp(t))^n.
The left side extends to an entire complex function because T has compact support.
For noninteger n the right side has a noninteger-order zero/branch at t=i*pi,
and cannot have that entire extension. Thus this exact bare-kernel interpretation
requires integer n. At integer n the binomial point-mass measure is the ordinary
construction; it still does not fix the layout-information counterexample above.
An observation-law normalizer dependent on theta changes the posterior.
For Lebesgue measure on f, that normalizer is
 Z_n(theta)=(theta^n-(1-theta)^n)/(n*log(theta/(1-theta))),
with Z_n(1/2)=2^(-n). Its theta dependence cannot be discarded in a Bayes factor.

3. A normalized conditional route exists once the real emissions are specified.
For a known layout D with C cells and binary latent off-cell indicators Z_c,
the exact weighted-mean law is
 p(F=x|theta,D)=sum_{z: sum_c N_c*z_c/N=x}
                theta^(sum z_c)*(1-theta)^(C-sum z_c).
Summing over all attainable x gives (theta+1-theta)^C=1.
With measurement noise, use normalized joint cell emissions g_S(Y_c|D),
g_O(Y_c|D) and, only when conditional independence is justified,
 p(Y|theta,D)=product_c ((1-theta)g_S(Y_c|D)+theta*g_O(Y_c|D)).
Shared-ray and pose nuisance variables must be integrated jointly before this
conditional product. Pixel reuse must not become independent cell evidence.
Using the same proper Jeffreys theta reference on [0,1] for S and its conditional
restriction theta>1/2 for M retains B=2*P(theta>1/2|Y,D) and B<=2.
The existing Beta-tail shortcut follows only for actual independent binary
observations with zero-error emissions. The mixture emissions are not yet
identified by the current f/n_eff return contract.

4. The quality/state/action obligations remain separate.
P05 counts reliable reconstruction cells whether currently viewed or not;
projected_physical_evidence.cpp bounds a finite selected population and applies
history-dependent inclusion. A Poisson family is normalized mathematically, but
the actual capped reliable count is not derived to have that sampling law.
K=30 is an inherited reconstruction requirement, not a measured movement rate.
The existing two q gates reduce algebraically to logit(q)+min(e,g)>0; this is
not q squared. A profile supremum over two null modes is not Bayesian integration,
and the low-coverage/same-state null mode remains a physical alternative.

5. Materialization has more than two classification outcomes.
Let p=P(M|Y), union costs (0,C_U) under (S,M), and old-only deferral costs
(C_V,C_N+C_G). The shared risk inequality for union is
 p*(C_U-C_N-C_G) <= (1-p)*C_V.
The current p<=1/2 choice requires a specific equality among these costs; it
does not derive them. Correct new-surface omission and retained old-site ghosts
are actual deferral costs. Original source/history constraints and atomic
handover restrict available actions and must be included in the same loss.
P08 I3 at 16.6 s and I13 at 18.7 s remove true new-site representation for one
output; P09 has one extra miss; P10 has recorded additional confirmed I11 ghost.
These adverse changes stay open under strict all-output acceptance.

Primary reference: Bissiri, Holmes, Walker, A general framework for updating belief
distributions (2016), https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.6430 . This paper provides coherent
loss-based belief updates; that establishes a different inference contract from
a generative measurement likelihood. Naming the existing kernel generalized
Bayes would not meet the requested common measurement-likelihood requirement.
The normalization and information proofs above are direct derivations for this
implementation, rather than claims imported from that paper.
