P05/P07 composition: normalized-family construction and remaining obligations

Let E denote reliable-copy coverage; G denote the geometric observation and D
the fixed observation design (cell layout/weights). Let f_E(E|lambda) be the
normalized Poisson family already used by P05. Let f_M(G|E,D), f_S(G|E,D)
be normalized conditional geometry laws. Conditional notation is essential:
it does not assume that coverage and geometry are independent observations.

Use the following common likelihood family over (E,G):
  L(E,G | lambda,h,D) = f_E(E|lambda) f_h(G|E,D), h in {M,S}.
For each fixed lambda,h this is normalized by iterated integration.

A two-mode composite comparison can reproduce the current min exactly:
  admissible moved-copy mode: lambda>=K, h=M;
  alternative mode U: lambda<K, h=M;
  alternative mode V: lambda>=K, h=S.
Write e=log(sup_{lambda>=K} f_E / sup_{lambda<K} f_E),
      g=log(f_M/f_S).
The numerator profiles over the moved-copy mode; the denominator profiles
over U OR V. Their likelihood ratio is min(exp(e),exp(g)).
Thus prior odds q/(1-q) enter ONCE and the existing conjunction is recovered.

This is a conditional construction, not a proof that the actual implementation
is an exact posterior or that the physical alternatives are exhaustive:
1. The normalization claim requires actual normalized f_M,f_S.
   P07 currently uses fractional effective counts; a fractional Bernoulli
   kernel alone does not specify a normalized observation law.
2. Profiling is maximization over nuisance parameters, not integration against
   a nuisance prior. q times a profile LR is a generalized posterior score.
3. If the physical null must include the additional independent combination
   lambda<K,h=S, its profile comparison becomes min(exp(e),exp(g),exp(e+g)).
   The current rule has not derived why the two-mode null is sufficient.
   This extra mode matters when both e and g are negative. It must not be
   deleted merely to recover current decisions.
4. Reliable coverage/geometry have a common source. A conditional law must
   be derived or estimated in the original measurement model; multiplying
   unrelated marginal scores does not meet that obligation.
5. A certificate of sufficient reconstruction is not automatically a physical
   transition variable. The common action loss must say why an insufficient
   changed copy leads to defer rather than assert that the state persisted.

No source code or accepted decision is changed by this construction.
It narrows the missing proof to explicit observation-law, composite-mode,
and nuisance-elimination questions. It does not add a new numeric parameter.
