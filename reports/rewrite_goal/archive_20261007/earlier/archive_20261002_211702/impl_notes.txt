4. README is silent; the most direct reading was implemented
-------------------------------------------------------------
 4.1  A "look" is one evidence round of a placement. A round contains a look iff it has a verdict (H or T) or a sample with an own-identity outcome. The status of a sample in a
      look is its newest informative frame: H with the placement's identity, H with another physical identity, T that does not land on the entity, or an entity-first-return reading
      (which supersedes older outcomes of that sample and carries no verdict: O). Samples with no informative frame carry nothing.
 4.2  The geometric test "within delta_* of another stored surface" uses a triangle grid of the measured mesh in world coordinates (cell = the element size) and the margin
      delta_+(rho, sigma_eff) + |b| of the sample; the extent test uses the same margin. An entity-first-return reading with a foreign or no label is O but not an identity hit.
 4.3  An entity-first-return own-identity reading counts as the sample's own-identity outcome for principle 6 (2) and for eps (labelled samples) but neither as h_e nor v_e of the sample.
 4.4  The identity term l^id is applied to every look that directly saw the placement in place, including one with no reliable verdict (weight 0, l^g = 0).
 4.5  The one-pass clearing is evaluated at the beginning of a look: reliable samples counted >= reliable total (the number of queried samples before the first directly seen look;
      afterwards the samples with h_e >= k_min and v_e = 0, for an inherited placement the queried samples less those with v_e > 0).
 4.6  Learning (先判后学) is the last step of a directly seen look: (n, F) of its reliable verdicts enter the in-place looks of the object (a look without reliable verdict adds none), the
      labelled/foreign counts enter eps, h_e/v_e/rho_e (running mean of the ranges of the own hits of the looks) are updated once per look.
 4.7  t_L = the earliest own-identity frame of the first look that directly saw the placement in place (a pending candidate keeps it while it is a candidate and hands it on when it is
      promoted or merged). A placement restored from the previous session, or never directly seen, starts at its presence begin (5f).
 4.8  A candidate's look is measured every round over the frames after its own watermark with the same rule as a placement (k_min from eps-hat). "Directly seen in >= k_min looks" counts
      rounds, not frames.
 4.9  (f): the candidate's surface samples are tested against the box of h's mesh expanded by delta_* at the range of the candidate's centre in the newest frame (plus |zeta_now - zeta_e| rho
      across sessions). h is "supported in those looks" if it directly saw itself in place in the same frames, or at least one of its samples read its own identity inside its extent, or it
      could not be judged yet (sigma_x unknown); this is sticky for the candidate. Established elsewhere and h unsupported: h closes, the candidate becomes current; established
      elsewhere and h supported: the candidate stays undecided and is neither folded in by (a), nor decided by (5o)/(g), nor merged for display.
 4.10 (g) is unchanged: all sampled cells of the candidate count as its reliable samples.
 4.11 Display merge (13 (e)): odds H_new : H_same <= 1 (q/(1-q) times exp of the evidence of both directions) merges a pending candidate for display; the registry keeps it pending.
 4.12 sigma_x window: W = max(h_o/2, sigma_table(rho)) + |zeta_now| rho + |zeta_e| rho_e (the upper bound of (12d)); the pairs are the readings (online) or the memory-to-present
      offsets (session end) inside W; the online reservoir keeps at most 4096 pairs (deterministic reservoir); the prior pair is the previous session's sigma_x or W/sqrt(3).
 4.13 sigma_table at the session end: signed residuals of the present vertices within one truncation fill 0.5 mm cells, those beyond count as far/near outliers of the EM of w_pm.
 4.14 Present faces as elements of (10b): centroid, normal, h = half voxel of the layer of the face (the identity of a face within one voxel of an object surface is that object, else
      background), tau = max(h, sigma_table), same footprint/hit/see-through relations as memory faces; their own building readings are hits. The class of the in-place distribution of
      a background face is -1 (no class: pooled looks of unclassified objects, else the cold start). A hidden present face is stored with its record (times, no colour/label).
 4.15 Tracker: sigma_c is estimated from the offset between the centroid of a physical object and the centroid of its overlapping motion cluster (E|offset|^2 = 3 sigma_c^2), v from the
      displacement of the associated pure dynamic tracks per second; chunk extraction applies to every track that is not pure dynamic.
 4.16 Frame pairs of the static selection: partner offsets 1, 2, 4, ... up to 64 frames (the scale-estimate frames), fused surface points per frame 16 bins x 1000 samples.
 4.17 RoundModel numerical guards: m clamped to (eps, 1 - eps) with eps the double epsilon; the look-to-look concentration is estimated from the objects with at least two looks
      and a positive spread (an object whose looks are all 0 carries no information about it, so zero-inflated data stays defined); the moment estimates are clamped to [0, 1]
      before the unit-information prior is added.
 4.18 Ground rules kept from round 4: samples are cells of the map resolution (<= 1500 per placement), class only enters through q and the class in-place distribution of the deletion
      (README principle 9), the native 0.3 m / 0.08 m ray tolerances are not used by the project decisions.

5. Computation budgets and performance (math-preserving)
---------------------------------------------------------
Budgets are those of README table 5.1 (1500 samples, 512 pixels, 64 directions, 64 frames / 16 pixels / 1000 samples, 300 observations); the others are listed in param_registry.csv
with the class "budget". Math-preserving optimisations: the session-end residuals are binned per chunk into signed cells and added in bulk (SensorCalibrator::addCells, equal to the
same addResidual calls); the triangle grid of an entity is built once per measurement; the reliable-total of a placement is counted once per look.
